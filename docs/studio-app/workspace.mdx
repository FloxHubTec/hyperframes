A project has three columns: the chat with your agent on the left, the preview in the middle, and panels on the
right. The timeline opens under the preview with **Show timeline**.

## The chat box: @, # and /

Three keys open menus right where you type.

<video className="hf-aside hf-spot" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/at-menu-v1.mp4" autoPlay muted loop playsInline aria-label="Typing @ opens the menu of things to add" />

**`@` adds something to your message.** The menu groups everything the agent can use: **Media** you added,
**Scenes & brand kit**, **Inspirations** (moments from real videos), **Captions** styles, **Voices**, other
**Projects**, or **Add files from your Mac**. Press **Tab** to jump between groups. Whatever you pick shows as a chip
above your words, so the agent knows exactly which file or style you mean.

<video className="hf-aside hf-spot" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/hash-tools-v1.mp4" autoPlay muted loop playsInline aria-label="Tools (#) under the chat box" />

**`#` picks a tool** for this request: **Storyboard**, **Brand kit**, **Podcast edit**, **HeyGen Voice 1**,
**Remove background**, **HeyGen Video 1**, **Reference video**, **Create assets** or **Computer use**. Use it when
you want a specific job done the specific way ("#Remove background on the presenter, then put the title behind her").
The HeyGen ones need a HeyGen API key.

<video className="hf-aside hf-spot" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/slash-menu-v1.mp4" autoPlay muted loop playsInline aria-label="Typing / opens commands and skills" />

**`/` runs a command or turns on a skill.** **/clear** starts a fresh chat (earlier messages stay above) and
**/compact** shortens a long one. Below them are ready-made video formats (founder take, AI breakdown reel, cards
behind you, behind-head captions and more) and any skills you have installed: picking one turns it on for the project.

<video className="hf-aside hf-spot" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/skills-menu-v1.mp4" autoPlay muted loop playsInline aria-label="The Skills menu in the chat header" />

The chat header shows which agent, model and effort the next request uses (click to change), and **Skills** lists
what the agent can use: HyperFrames' own (always on), **Talking-head formats** and **Video workflows** that work only
when picked, and **Your skills** from Claude Code or Codex.

## Script: edit the video like a document

Open **Script** at the top of the right panel. Every word spoken in your video appears as text, transcribed on your
computer. Select words (click the first, Shift-click the last) and press **Delete**: the video is cut with them. The
panel says how much shorter it got, and **Put back** undoes any cut.

*Select a sentence, Delete: "1 cut · 4.9 s shorter", and playback jumps straight over it.*

With words selected, the bar also offers **Play** (just those words), **Silence** (keep the picture, mute the
sound), **Clip** (cut them into a vertical clip of their own) and **Fix** (retype a word; it's spoken again in the same
voice). **Clean up** removes **Ums and long pauses**, or **Repeated takes**, keeping the last take of each line you
said more than once.

## Framey

Framey, the small green character by the preview, shows what your agent is doing and tells you when a video is ready
or needs you. Change how it looks in **Settings** > **Framey**.

## The preview tools

Under the preview: **Select** to click any element (right-click it for **Ask Framey**, layer order, **Cut out object
here** on footage), **Draw** to circle what should change, and **Comment** to leave a note at a moment. Changes stack
up and go to the agent together; see [edit a video with the agent](/studio-app/edit-with-the-agent).
