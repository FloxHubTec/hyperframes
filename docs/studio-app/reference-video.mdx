Found a video whose editing you love? Give it to the Studio app. Your video gets its cuts, captions and effects, with
your own people, words and brand in it. Showing a video once is faster than describing it, and a video you only half
like is still a better start than a blank page.

*An uploaded video becomes the reference. Choose **Copy editing style**, then an AI avatar, then what the video should
say.*

Reference uses your HeyGen API key: the app watches the video through HeyGen, which uses your API balance. Add the
key in **Settings** > **HeyGen API key**, or when the app asks.

## 1. Pick the video

On Home, open **Reference**. Then:

- **Paste a link.** One public video from YouTube (a video or a Short), TikTok, Instagram or X, up to 30 minutes, or a
  published HyperFrames project.
- **Upload a file** from your computer.
- **Pick one from the feed.** **Remix from inspiration** on Home is a feed of videos made to be remixed.

## 2. Copy the editing, or the motion

Choose what to take from it:

- **Copy editing style:** your video gets its cuts, captions and effects, with your footage or an AI avatar.
- **Motion graphics:** recreate its graphics and animation, with nobody on camera.

Then choose **Continue**.

## 3. Say who's on camera

For **Copy editing style**, the app asks **Who's on camera?**

- **You:** upload your own takes. Add **Editing instructions** if you want, for example "Cut the pauses, keep it under
  30 seconds".
- **An AI avatar:** your photo or a HeyGen presenter. Say what the avatar should talk about; a sentence or two is
  enough.

## 4. Swap what should be yours

The app lists the parts of the reference you can swap: the person, a product, the voice, the music, the words on
screen, and anything else you name, such as an outfit or a background. Remove one to keep it as it is in the
reference. For the rest, choose how to fill each:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Framey writes it** / **Framey decides** | Framey fills it to fit your topic |
| **Upload** | Use your own file |
| **Generate** | Make a picture or clip. The price shows before you choose it |
| **Type yours** | Write the exact words |

Choose a brand kit for the video, or **No brand kit**. Your workspace default stays as you set it. Framey may ask a
question or two about anything the video leaves open.

## 5. Compare with the original

While Framey builds, **Show original** at the top of the preview plays the reference over the picture. When the
build is done, the same button sets the two side by side, on one clock, so you can see what matches and what doesn't.
Then [edit the result](/studio-app/edit-with-the-agent) like any other video.

## Use other people's videos fairly

The app copies the editing style, not the footage: your video is built from your own takes, avatar, words and
media. Don't reuse the original's footage, music or logos unless you have the rights to them.
